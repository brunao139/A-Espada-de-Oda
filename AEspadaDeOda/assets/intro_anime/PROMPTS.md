## youkai_preparation.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: cinematic anime keyframe for the original video game A Espada de Oda, 16:9 landscape. The supplied image is the strict character identity reference, NOT the requested pose or background. Preserve Youkai's exact original segmented gold armor, very dark green-black undersuit, enclosed black helmet, long orange V-shaped horns, gold circular ear armor, angular chest plates, dark gloves, one katana with gold guard. No exposed human face, no redesign, no extra limbs or swords. Create a modern high-end hand-drawn 2D shounen anime film still with expressive crisp ink lines, sophisticated cel shading, chiaroscuro, dazzling cyan-magenta neon reflections and restrained luminous golden aura. Scene: rainy cyberpunk rooftop at night. Medium-wide three-quarter frontal view, Youkai takes a low, focused sword-draw preparation stance, left hand holding the scabbard at the hip, right hand firmly on katana grip still mostly sheathed, shoulders coiled, eyes starting to glow cyan. Body has believable anatomy. He looks intently toward the camera, ready to cut toward the lens in the following shot. Camera low and slightly canted, strong cinematic foreshortening, whole helmet and both hands visible, character occupies right-center with blade draw space to the left. Fine rain streaks, reflective rooftop, immense distant buildings and holographic abstract signage, turquoise moon through mist. Thin golden ki ribbons spiral around his arms and scabbard as in reference; not overwhelming pose. No pixel art, no 3D render, no UI, no typography, no watermark. Single coherent film shot, not a storyboard grid.

## menu_background.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: final static background plate for a modern high-end 2D shounen anime game title menu, wide landscape 16:9. Original scene combining two eras of Japan at night: a grand traditional Japanese castle and layered curved tiled rooftops on the LEFT, transforming through a subtle vertical fracture of luminous gold particles into an immense futuristic cyberpunk skyline on the RIGHT. Full turquoise moon high in mist, cyan and violet illuminated skyscrapers, subtle magenta holographic rectangular lights with NO readable text, warm small paper lanterns among the old roofs, fine rain in foreground. Strong cinematic atmosphere, beautiful hand-painted anime environment art with precise architectural linework, chiaroscuro, layered depth, gold versus cyan-violet light. Horizon and focal moon in upper third. Central portion is dark calm negative space suitable for a large game title and three buttons to be added by code; keep lower center free of prominent objects. Frame with dark rooftop silhouettes at bottom corners. No characters, no swords, no typography, no logos, no UI, no buttons, no watermark. Not pixel art, not photorealism, not 3D. This is the clean background AFTER the hero has cut the camera with a white flash.
